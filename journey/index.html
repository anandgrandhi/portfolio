Experience

A journey through different stages of business.

From building products during hypergrowth to operating at enterprise scale, founding a business and applying AI to new problems.

2015–2020 · BYJU’S

From app development to global scale.

I joined BYJU’S before the launch of its flagship learning app. Over the next five years, I led multiple verticals, contributing to the company’s rapid growth through the expansion of its K–12 product portfolio, interactive STEM experiences, personalised learning systems and large-scale production capabilities.

2020–2023 · Amazon

Building Amazon’s education business in India.

At Amazon Academy, my responsibilities spanned product initiatives, customer experience and business operations. My work focused on increasing customer engagement, driving product adoption, designing personalised experiences, improving customer support mechanisms and establishing operational systems.

2022–present · ARCHMATION

Building a business around solving problems.

Through ARCHMATION, I moved beyond the responsibilities of a product leader into running a business — acquiring clients, understanding their requirements, managing delivery and improving margins. As generative AI matured, we began using it to reduce production costs, automate repetitive work and develop new solutions for our clients.

Present day · Applied AI

Solving business problems with AI.

My recent work focuses on applying AI to institutional knowledge management, employee productivity, customer acquisition and decision support. I work across business discovery, product strategy, solution design and evaluation, collaborating with technical teams on practical AI implementations.
