Experience

My professional journey.

2015–2020 · BYJU’S

Building the foundations for scale.

I joined BYJU'S before the launch of its flagship learning app. Over five years, my responsibilities expanded from leading specialised teams to owning a product category during its international expansion. I worked across new product development, knowledge architecture and interactive learning, building the teams and systems needed to support rapid growth. This experience shaped my understanding of what it takes to build and scale products.

2020–2022 · Amazon

Building Amazon’s EdTech business in India.

At Amazon, I brought that experience into a more customer- and data-driven product environment. I led initiatives across five product areas, working at the intersection of product development, customer experience and business operations. Understanding customer behaviour, identifying opportunities through data and translating them into measurable product improvements became central to my approach.

2022–September 2026 · ARCHMATION

From product leadership to entrepreneurship.

Co-founding ARCHMATION broadened my perspective from building products to building a business. Working directly with founders and business leaders, I identified commercial opportunities, built multidisciplinary teams and developed technology-led solutions across industries. It gave me a deeper understanding of how to evaluate technology through business value, cost and feasibility, particularly as AI began transforming business operations.

Present

Making AI operational.

Today, I bring together these experiences — building products at scale, solving customer problems and running a business — to explore how AI can improve the way organisations work. My focus is on designing intelligent systems that connect organisational knowledge, decision-making and execution.
